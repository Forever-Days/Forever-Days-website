About Forever Days

毎日に、長く愛せる
“いいもの”を。

Forever Daysは、FD Global Tradingが運営する日本発のオンラインショップです。

私たちは、日本ならではの魅力ある商品を厳選し、海外のお客様へ安心してお買い物を楽しんでいただけるサービスを届けることを目指しています。

日本と世界をつなぎ、商品との出会いがその先の毎日を少し豊かにすること。

ABOUT FOREVER DAYS

Quality for the days that matter.

Forever Days is a Japan-based online store operated by FD Global Trading.

We carefully select products that reflect the unique appeal of Japan, with the goal of providing customers around the world with a reliable and enjoyable shopping experience.

At the heart of everything we do are three values: Trust, Quality, and Care.

We believe our role goes beyond simply selling products. We approach every customer and every order with sincerity, striving to build lasting trust through dependable service and thoughtful attention to detail.

By connecting Japan with the world, we hope each new discovery brings a little more joy and quality to everyday life.

That is the promise of Forever Days.

— FD Global Trading
